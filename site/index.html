クラフト Server panel for iPhone

Your game servers.
In your pocket.

Kurafuto is a native iPhone app for your self-hosted server panel: start and stop servers, watch the live console, manage players, schedules, backups and files – from anywhere.

Coming soon to the App Store

機能 Features

Everything your panel does, native on iPhone.

  • 一

    Live console

    Follow your server in real time and send commands with quick actions and history.

  • 二

    Players & backups

    Whitelist, kick or ban players. Create, restore and clean up backups.

  • 三

    Schedules & files

    Plan restarts and backups, edit configuration files, manage webhooks.

  • 四

    Users & roles

    Give friends exactly the access they need, server by server.

  • 五

    Widgets & Siri

    See status and players on your Home and Lock Screen. Start or stop servers with Siri and Shortcuts.

  • 六

    Face ID lock

    Lock the app, and confirm stopping, deleting or restoring with Face ID.

画面 Screens

A closer look.

  1. 一

    Welcome

    Try every screen with sample data, or connect your own Crafty Controller panel with a password, a 2FA code or an API token. Self-signed certificates are trusted only after you check their fingerprint.

  2. 二

    One server

    Start, restart and stop with a confirmation, live gauges for CPU, RAM and world size, and the last minutes as a chart. Console, logs, players, schedules, backups and files are one tap away.

  3. 三

    Live console

    Follow the server log as it happens over the panel's own connection, send commands, and pick from quick commands or your history.

  4. 四

    Dark or light

    Choose a mode and an accent color in Settings. Sakura pink is the default; CPU and RAM follow your accent.

安心 Privacy

Private by design

守

  • No tracking, no analytics, no ads, no accounts.
  • The app talks only to the panel you add – never to us or anyone else.
  • Your token stays in the iOS Keychain on your device.
  • No third-party code – only Apple’s own frameworks.
